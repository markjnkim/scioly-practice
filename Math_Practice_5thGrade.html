💡 Fraction & Decimal Strategies

  1. Common denominator trick: Before adding or subtracting fractions, find the LCD. Ask: "What number do both denominators divide into evenly?"
  2. Fraction to decimal: Divide the top by the bottom. 3/4 → 3 ÷ 4 = 0.75.
  3. Multiply/divide by powers of 10: Moving the decimal right = ×10. Moving left = ÷10.
  4. Compare fractions fast: Convert everything to decimals first, then rank them.

🧮 Word Problem Strategy

  1. Read twice, solve once. Read all the way through before writing anything. Underline the question.
  2. Circle the key numbers and label what they represent.
  3. Choose your operation: "How many groups?" = divide. "Total if repeated?" = multiply.
  4. Check with estimation before committing to an answer.

📐 Geometry Memory Tricks

📦Area vs. Volume: Area = flat surface (cm²). Volume = fills a 3D box (cm³).
🔺Triangle angles always = 180°: Subtract the two known angles from 180 to find the third.
🔵Circle terms: Diameter = all the way across. Radius = half of diameter.

🔢 Number Sense Quick Wins

⚡Prime number check: Try dividing by 2, 3, 5, 7. If none work and the number is under 100, it's prime!
🔁LCM vs GCF: LCM = smallest number both divide into (go UP). GCF = biggest number that divides into both (go DOWN).
📈Patterns: Look at what happens between terms — is it +5? ×2? −3? Find the rule, apply it.
